- No linked-device login, QR pairing, send/receive round-trip, or live Business credential was tested.
- Meta Groups developer pages returned HTTP 429; API eligibility, scopes and limits for the intended WABA remain unverified.
- The WAHA image was not pulled or run. Image digest metadata is not a startup test or full image SBOM.
- The source map tracks repository-level license evidence, not every transitive dependency or redistribution obligation.
